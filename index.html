GET TO KNOW OPOL

Where Community
Meets Opportunity

Opol is a coastal municipality in Misamis Oriental, located near Cagayan de Oro City. Its coastal scenery, local communities, and growing development make it an important part of Northern Mindanao.

This website concept brings together information about the municipality, public services, local attractions, and community updates in one accessible place.
